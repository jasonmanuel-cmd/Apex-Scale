02 — The protocol

The Apex Multiplier
Protocol™

One master asset. Three movements. A distribution grid that behaves less like a marketing campaign and more like infrastructure.

01 / 03

Pillar Asset ExtractionTwo hours in, an archive out

We ingest your podcast, keynote, or a guided two-hour session and mine it for high-signal moments. Every cut is hook-engineered for the first three seconds and reviewed by a human editor before it ever reaches a feed.

  • First-three-second retention architecture
  • Dual-layer editorial review — machine draft, human finish
  • Bespoke typography, sound design and grading per brand
02 / 03

The Sub-Account Swarm™Institutional distribution

Twenty to forty branded sub-accounts — clipping channels, topical editions, regional arms — operated by full-time, US-managed social teams. Each account earns its own reach, so throttling one never silences the portfolio.

  • 100% platform-guideline compliant, account by account
  • Zero exposure to your personal verified profile
  • Up to 2,400 published posts per month
03 / 03

Funnel RoutingViews are not the deliverable

Reach without routing is vanity. Every account carries a defined path back to your flagship profile, list, book, or calendar — measured weekly against qualified inbound, not impressions.

  • Profile and link architecture engineered per account
  • Lead-magnet gateways with SMS and email capture
  • Performance guarantee enforced against verified platform analytics
